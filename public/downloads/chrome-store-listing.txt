Detailed description
--------------------
FIGHURAI Colors brings the same Colors controls from fighur.ai to any website.

• Change background and text colors while you browse
• Floating Colors button + toolbar popup
• Synced with your FIGHURAI Pro plan (entitlement only — no email/name shared with the extension)
• Pin the extension, open any site, click Colors

Requires an active FIGHURAI Pro subscription. Sign in at https://fighur.ai then use the extension.
